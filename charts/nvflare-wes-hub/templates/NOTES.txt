The hub {{ .Values.hubName }} is starting. Sandbox only.

1. The Service shows the internal address; ip_pairs pairs it with the public
   one. Point an A record for {{ .Values.hubName }} to the public one:

     oc get service {{ include "hub.fullname" . }}-fl -o jsonpath='{.status.loadBalancer.ingress[0].ip}'
     oc get ipaddresspools.metallb.io -n metallb-system {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.metadata.annotations.ip_pairs}'

2. Kits: https://{{ .Values.kitsHost }}/kits (only this path). Certificate
   signing requests: https://{{ .Values.kitsHost }}/sites (only this path).
   Both Routes must say Admitted=True:

     oc get route {{ include "hub.fullname" . }}-kits -o jsonpath='{.status.ingress[0].conditions[0].type}={.status.ingress[0].conditions[0].status}'
     oc get route {{ include "hub.fullname" . }}-csr -o jsonpath='{.status.ingress[0].conditions[0].type}={.status.ingress[0].conditions[0].status}'

3. The WES is not exposed. Reach it with:

     oc port-forward service/{{ include "hub.fullname" . }}-wes 8080:8080

   then http://localhost:8080/ga4gh/wes/v1/service-info

The pod waits for the Secret {{ .Values.sitesSecret }} with the key sites.json.
